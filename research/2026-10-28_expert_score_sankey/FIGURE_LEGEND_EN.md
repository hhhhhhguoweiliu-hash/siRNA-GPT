**Figure. Distribution of expert-assigned score points across model configurations, questions and evaluation dimensions.** The left column contains Agent, fixed workflow and LLM configurations; the middle column contains the nine seen development questions in their original order; the right column contains factual accuracy, evidence support, requirement completion and boundary handling. Ribbons from a configuration to a question represent the sum of its four ordinal dimension scores (each 0-2; total 0-8). Ribbons from a question to a dimension represent the corresponding original item score, retaining model identity by color. All ribbons use the same linear point-to-width scale, with equal incoming and outgoing score totals at every question. Zero-score ribbons have zero width and are omitted graphically; all 108 item scores remain in the source and exported data. Model labels show cumulative scores over nine questions (maximum 72); dimension labels show cumulative scores over three configurations and nine questions (maximum 54). Scores come from one user-confirmed human expert, including the documented user correction of the Q9 transcription error, as recorded at repository commit c68baaf2a47b91c9713fc1c046f2bbfa45b217c8. Q1-Q4 are standard tasks, Q5 is a requirement-change task and Q6-Q9 are reliability tasks. This is a descriptive allocation of awarded ordinal points, not a causal flow, classification accuracy estimate, independent unseen-task evaluation or measure of biological efficacy. The original rating scope for Q3, including the distinction between the main response and appended model/tool evidence, remains as documented in the source review.
