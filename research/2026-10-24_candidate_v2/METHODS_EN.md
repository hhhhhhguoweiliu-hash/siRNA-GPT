# Revised candidate workflow: actual local execution

The revised workflow was defined before inference in PROTOCOL.md. Human VEGFA NM_003376.6 was the primary design reference for chemically synthesized siRNA. The 20 fixed reference transcripts were retained for transparent coverage reporting, rather than requiring every design to match all 20. Reference coverage was not interpreted as experimentally demonstrated target-region expression in ARPE-19 cells. The development set is not an unseen test set or an efficacy-labelled experimental cohort.

All 104 previously preserved siDirect 2.0 designs were reconsidered without resubmitting the design service. The original request enabled the Ui-Tei sequence rule and seed melting-temperature filter. The revised pool retained designs with a recorded minimum non-target mismatch count of at least two for both strands at guide/passenger positions 2-20, yielding 65 designs. The exact historical siDirect database release remains unknown. Original rounded melting temperatures, including a printed 21.5 degrees C boundary, were retained and flagged rather than reinterpreted using unobserved internal precision. The previous project GC, four-base homopolymer and all-transcript conditions were recorded as historical annotations, not additional hard filters for the complete revised pool. OligoFormer sequence warnings were reported using the original official function; this does not validate an optimal combination of filters.

The original 21-nt duplex strings and native terminal bases were preserved. A paired 19-nt core and 57-nt target mRNA context were derived using previously verified strand/coordinate relationships. Core coordinates are one-based and inclusive and are distinct from the 23-nt siDirect design window and from the 19-nt positions-2-20 near-full search window. The 65 designs comprise five coding-region, nine 5prime-UTR/boundary and 51 3prime-UTR designs on the primary reference, whose annotated coding sequence spans positions 497-1684.

The separately purchased si-VEGF was recovered from the user-designated original synthesis DOCX. Its two strings, GAUAGAGCAAGACAAGAAATT with the documented N3 suffix and UUUCUUGUCUUGCUCUAUCTT, were retained unchanged. Under an explicitly conditional 5prime-to-3prime interpretation of the written strand order, the complementary 19-base paired cores map to positions 1439-1457 of NM_003376.6. The second written core was used as the putative guide for model input; this derived assignment is not vendor confirmation of strand roles or terminal chemistry. Predictions concern the unmodified sequence core and reference context, and cannot establish the efficacy of the N3-modified or conjugated product. The purchased entry was kept separate from the revised design pool and literature references. Chen's reported 19-base target sequence and Osipova's reported 27-bp duplex were retained as literature evidence without inventing missing strands or automatically trimming the 27-bp input for this model.

OligoFormer was executed locally on CPU using the unchanged official model/features at commit e2f53ad63387bbe166bf123949151e2bc9bf6ec3 and verified original weights. The adapter retained the previously documented explicit eval/no_grad setting, deterministic seed 42, two CPU threads, the official RNA-FM layer-12 token slice, and official input features. Official example inputs were evaluated with repeated identical predictions, and the adapter tensor layout was checked against the official Dataset/DataLoader. The actual environment was Python 3.8.16, torch 2.2.1+cpu, numpy 1.24.4 and pandas 2.0.3. New inference covered all 65 designs and the separately purchased sequence. Pilot and remaining phases were disjoint input subsets and jointly covered 66 unique entries; earlier failed adapter checks were retained. Outputs were multiplied by the official factor 1.341; values may exceed one and are neither calibrated probabilities nor measured knockdown percentages. The nine historical control inputs reproduced their saved scores and sequence warnings exactly. PITA, TargetScan, toxicity and the mismatch model were not executed, so this is not a reproduction of every upstream module.

SeedMatchR 1.1.1 was executed by sourcing its two unchanged official core-function files in R 4.3.1, using Biostrings 2.70.1. The documented get_seed example was run first. The reference was the retained GRCh38.p14/RefSeq RS_2025_08 representative 3prime-UTR set: one transcript per gene for 19,415 genes. Both strands of all 66 entries were processed for mer7m8, zero mismatches and no indels. Extraction of positions 2-8 from the paired core preserves the corresponding seed of the original 21-character strand and does not reassign TT chemistry. All 2,562,780 gene-count rows, including zeros, were preserved. Each saved positive site was checked against the actual reference sequence and summed back to the official gene counts. The historical nine-design positive guide subset reproduced all 14,524 saved entries exactly. These matches are potential sequence interactions, not observed gene repression or toxicity.

A separate near-full sequence check used the official Biostrings vmatchPattern/neditStartingAt functions against all 186,185 retained RefSeq RNA records, allowing at most two mismatches and no indels. Both paired-core and positions-2-20 windows were searched for both strands. This transparent supplemental scan does not reproduce the historical siDirect server algorithm/database. Transcript hits, positions, mismatch counts and gene mapping were preserved, with VEGFA, non-target genes and unknown mappings distinguished. Pilot timings projected approximately 26.2 minutes for the broad 264-query plan, exceeding the prospectively stated 15-minute limit. The bounded fallback therefore completed the five coding-region designs and purchased sequence, 24 queries in total. The remaining 240 UTR queries were marked NOT_RUN_BUDGET_SCOPE, never zero, and those designs were not certified clear. All sites in the completed scope were independently rechecked and gene summaries recounted.

A provisional review list was generated only within the completely checked coding-region group. Designs with official sequence flag zero and no mapped non-target or unknown-mapping near match at zero or one mismatch were considered in descending official model score, retaining non-overlapping paired-core target regions up to a maximum of three. This produced two entries, VEGFA_SD_1555 and VEGFA_SD_1157. The complete 65-design table still retains all warnings and all excluded-from-shortlist entries. The purchased sequence is a separate reference, not an automatically preferred design. No seed-count cutoff, state-related weighting or fused risk score was introduced. Coverage of only 14/20 fixed transcripts for 1555, compared with 20/20 for 1157 and 18/20 for the purchased sequence, requires a biological target-scope decision. Historical transcript TPM values were checked against the retained vendor table and supplied only as background, not as direct target-region read coverage or perturbed-expression evidence.

The three specified experimental papers and the single real day-7 no-answer regression were independently rechecked and reused from the preceding delivery. That regression contained one actual model exchange and explicitly stated the absence of the requested project measurement; it was not rerun or counted as a new independent case. No new paid Q&A call, wet experiment, HIF extension, independent human scoring or old 54-record rerun was performed. The new offline records do not replace the production website's original nine-design data or demonstrate clinical benefit, safety, generalization or superiority.
