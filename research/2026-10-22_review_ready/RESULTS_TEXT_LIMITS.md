# 可供论文草稿使用的限定结果表述

以下英文只描述实际运行与材料核验，不替代未完成的独立质量评分。

> We recorded all 54 prespecified task–configuration–repeat observations across six previously seen development tasks and three system configurations. The 80 recorded model requests returned completed responses, and the original answers, inputs, tool evidence, and provider-reported usage were retained. Two Agent observations ended with generic failure messages despite recorded input-check errors; these original outcomes were retained without replacement. The material was prepared for independent review, with all quality-score fields left blank.

> The no-tool model, fixed workflow, and Agent received the same original task inputs but had different execution resources. The fixed workflow used a human-selected path, whereas the Agent could plan and make multiple model requests. Scientific scores and seed-match results were imported historical outputs, and the expression data represented untreated baseline cultures from a single culture run. These development results establish traceable execution records; they do not establish superior scientific accuracy, siRNA knockdown efficacy, experimental off-target safety, or generalization to unseen tasks.
