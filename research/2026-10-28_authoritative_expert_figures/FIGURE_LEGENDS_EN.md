# Figure legends

All figures use one user-confirmed domain expert's ratings of the same 27 original responses to nine seen development tasks. Each dimension is scored from 0 to 2. Total ratings are the four equally weighted dimensions summed to 0-8. The user's Q9 transcription correction is applied: both Workflow A011 and Agent A025 receive 8/8. No other ratings were changed.

**Figure 1. Overall and dimension-specific expert ratings.** (a) Mean total rating for each configuration across nine tasks. (b) Mean factual accuracy, evidence support, requirement completion, and boundary handling ratings. Points show individual task ratings; they are not independent repeat runs or additional raters. Agent mean total rating was 7.78, compared with 5.78 for LLM and 5.67 for Workflow.

**Figure 2. Radar plot of the four mean dimension ratings.** The underlying values are identical to Figure 1b, on the original 0-2 scale. Agent received higher evidence support and requirement completion ratings. LLM factual accuracy was slightly higher (1.89 versus 1.78); LLM and Agent tied on boundary handling (2.00). Polygon area is not an additional metric.

**Figure 3. Total rating for every task.** Q1-Q4 are standard tasks, Q5 is a two-round requirement-change episode, and Q6-Q9 are reliability tasks. Each bar is one response's total expert rating. Agent exceeded LLM for all nine tasks and tied Workflow on Q4 and Q9. The two rounds in an episode are not counted as separate tasks.

**Figure 4. Total ratings by task category.** Bars show category means and points show task ratings. There are four standard tasks, one requirement-change task, and four reliability tasks per configuration. The single requirement-change task does not support a generalizable estimate for that category.

**Figure 5. Matrix of all original dimension ratings.** Rows represent Q1-Q9; columns represent the four dimensions. The three configurations share the same 0-2 color scale. All 108 scores are displayed after the two user-authorized Q9 entry corrections. Blank or failed analyses were not synthesized as new scores.

**Figure 6. Within-task differences in total rating.** Each bar is the Agent rating minus the corresponding LLM or Workflow rating for the same task. Zero indicates a tie, including Agent-Workflow comparisons on Q4 and Q9. Q6 and Q7 account for 11/19 of the cumulative Agent-Workflow total-point difference.

These plots describe ordinal expert ratings of evidence retrieval and response delivery. They do not establish biological efficacy, general scientific reasoning superiority, or classification Accuracy, Precision, Recall, or F1. The original run included shared validation/error presentation; in Q9 the identical final reports originated from shared error-report code. The scope used to rate A013's raw model text versus its delivered main answer remains a methods detail to document. No new model calls or new quality ratings were produced.
